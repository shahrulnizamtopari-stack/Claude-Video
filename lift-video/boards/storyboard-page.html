The 8 segments

01: Kabel putus
0–6s · still at 5.2s

Hook. Close on the lift at floor 30, a person inside, cables taut above. On "putus" one cable snaps with a spark burst, the car jolts and the indicator flashes red.

“Bayangkan kau dalam lif, tingkat tiga puluh. Tiba-tiba, kabel lif putus.”

Sound: low hum of the shaft; sharp metallic SNAP + spark crackle; car jolt thud

Camera: 0–6: front of the car at floor 30, slow push-in; jolt on the snap.

02: Bukan satu kabel
6–14s · still at 11.5s

Camera rises along the cables to the roof machine room. The 5 remaining cables light up cyan one by one as they are counted.

“Tenang. Lif tu takkan terus jatuh. Sebab dia bukan tergantung pada satu kabel je. Biasanya ada empat sampai lapan kabel keluli.”

Sound: soft tick per cable lighting up

Camera: 6–14: rise and pull back along the cables to the machine room.

03: Satu pun cukup
14–21s · still at 18.8s

Back down at the car: five more people pop in. All cables dim except one, which glows yellow; an orange WEIGHT arrow points down, a yellow arrow up on the one cable.

“Dan satu kabel pun biasanya dah cukup kuat untuk tampung lif yang penuh orang.”

Sound: pop per person; low whoomp on the arrows

Camera: 14–21: drift back down to the car, eye level with the people.

04: Governor
21–31s · still at 28.3s

Every remaining cable snaps (sparks), the car starts to fall in slow motion and the m/s dial appears. Camera whips up to the governor on the roof: its wheel spins faster, the fly-weights swing out glowing, the pawl catches and the governor rope locks.

“Tapi katakanlah semua kabel putus. Kat atas sekali ada roda kecil namanya governor. Bila lif jatuh terlalu laju, pemberat dalam roda tu tercampak keluar dan terus kunci tali dia.”

Sound: rapid snaps; rising whine of the spinning wheel; hard CLACK on the lock

Camera: 21–31: fast whip up the shaft to the governor; close 3/4 view of the wheel.

05: Baji cengkam rel
31–41s · still at 34.0s

Down the glowing governor rope to the falling car: the lever pulls, steel wedges slide up and clamp both rails, sparks shower, the dial drops from red to 0 and the car stops a few floors lower.

“Tali yang terkunci tu tarik tuas kat lif. Baji besi terus cengkam rel kiri dan kanan, dan lif berhenti dalam jarak yang pendek.”

Sound: metal screech + spark sizzle fading to a stop; relieved silence

Camera: 31–41: dive down the governor rope to the falling car; follow it to its stop.

06: Spring di dasar
41–48s · still at 45.5s

Camera dives to the pit at the bottom of the shaft (cut-away podium). Two big springs pop on; a see-through ghost car drops onto them to show how they soften an over-run.

“Kat dasar lubang lif pula ada spring besar, untuk lembutkan hentakan kalau lif terlajak ke bawah.”

Sound: boing + soft thud, dust puff

Camera: 41–48: dive to the pit through the cut-away podium.

07: Otis, 1854
48–55s · still at 52.3s

Out on the island an old wooden hoist builds itself. A man in a top hat stands on the platform, an axe cuts the rope, the platform drops a little and the safety catches the teeth; he lifts his hat.

“Brek keselamatan ni dicipta Elisha Otis. Tahun 1854, dia berdiri atas pelantar tinggi, suruh orang potong talinya, dan pelantar tu tak jatuh.”

Sound: axe chop, rope snap, ratchet CLICK, small crowd gasp-to-cheer (synthesised)

Camera: 48–55: out onto the island, a 3/4 view of the 1854 hoist.

08: Paling selamat
55–60s · still at 57.4s

Pull back to the whole tower on the floating island at sunset, then push straight back into the lift: the last frame matches the first, so it loops.

“Sebab tu lif antara pengangkutan paling selamat dalam dunia.”

Sound: warm swell, chime; ends on the shaft hum from frame 0

Camera: 55–60: pull back to the whole tower at sunset, then push straight into the lift. The last frame matches the first.

Risks and unclear points

  1. The script is my draft. Approve or edit the wording before you record it. Timings are placeholders until then.
  2. Facts. Cables: commonly 4–8 steel ropes on a traction lift (some have 3 or more than 8). "Satu kabel pun biasanya cukup" is a simplification: each rope set has a large safety factor, which is why the line says "biasanya". The governor (centrifugal fly-weights trip a jaw that grips the governor rope), wedge safeties on the rails, and buffers for *over-run* (not free fall) are all accurate. Otis did demonstrate his safety brake in New York in 1854 by having the hoist rope cut.
  3. Slow motion. The fall is shown slowed down; the m/s numbers on the dial are illustrative, in a realistic range.
  4. The hook arrives at about 5 s (on "putus"). For a stronger hook, the snap could come earlier if the first line is shorter, e.g. "Tingkat tiga puluh. Kabel lif… putus." Your call.
  5. Loop. The broken lift is swapped for an intact one at floor 30 while the camera is out on the island.
  6. Your subtitles. The subject sits in the upper-middle of the frame, so the centre and lower-middle stay free for subtitles. Tell me if you put subtitles somewhere else.
  7. Render time. The v2 effects are about 2× heavier: the 540×960 preview takes roughly 12 min, the final roughly 30–40 min.
  8. Sound. Synthesised effects (snap, sparks, governor whine, lock clack, screech, springs, axe, ratchet). No music: add a trending sound in the TikTok app.

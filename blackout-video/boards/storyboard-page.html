Macam Mana Kalau Satu Malaysia Blackout 7 Hari?

The 8 segments

01: Blackout
0–6s · still at 5.0 s

Hook. The street at sunset, every window and lamp lit, a fan turning. At 2.8 s the lights die in a wave towards the camera and the sky drops to night. At 4.6 s one house at the end flickers back on.

“Macam mana kalau satu Malaysia blackout, tujuh hari? Semua gelap. Kecuali satu rumah.”

Sound: evening hum; heavy power-down thunk, lamps ticking off; one soft click as the last house relights

Camera: 0–6: high wide shot down the street at sunset, slow push-in; the blackout wave runs towards the camera, then one house flickers back on.

02: Hari 1: lilin
6–13s · still at 10.6 s

HARI 1. Camera comes down to street level. Candles pop on outside each house, neighbours stand outside with their phones still glowing.

“Hari pertama, semua orang relaks lagi. Pasang lilin, lepak depan rumah, rasa macam camping.”

Sound: crickets; a match strike and soft pop per candle

Camera: 6–13: descend to street level and drift along the candles and people.

03: Hari 2: telefon mati
13–21s · still at 17.6 s

HARI 2. Close on one neighbour's phone: battery 15 % → 0 %, the bar turns red, the screen goes black. Behind, the other phones wink out one by one.

“Hari kedua, telefon mula mati. Powerbank pun habis. Tak ada line, tak ada berita, tak tahu apa jadi kat luar.”

Sound: low-battery beeps getting slower; power-off blip per phone

Camera: 13–21: close on one resident's phone as it drains, slow move past them.

04: Hari 3: peti ais
21–29s · still at 26.0 s

HARI 3. Into a dark house. The fridge skin turns see-through: food fades from fresh colours to brown-green, a puddle spreads, green smell puffs rise.

“Hari ketiga, makanan dalam peti ais mula rosak. Malam pula panas, gelap, susah nak tidur.”

Sound: drip, drip; a low sour drone; a mosquito whine

Camera: 21–29: turn into the middle-left house, push in on the see-through fridge.

05: Satu rumah menyala
29–37s · still at 33.6 s

Camera rises over the dark street and travels to the one house that still glows warm: lamp on, phones on the table all green.

“Tapi rumah yang satu tu? Dah tiga malam, lampu dia masih menyala. Telefon semua penuh.”

Sound: wind drops out; warm pad swells as the house comes into view

Camera: 29–37: rise and look down the dark street to the one lit house, then travel towards it.

06: Kotak ni
37–47s · still at 42.8 s

The roof fades away. The black box on the floor gets a yellow outline and its skin turns see-through, showing the battery cells glowing. Three cables light up with moving pulses: to the lamp, to the phones, to the fan, which starts to spin.

“Rahsia dia, kotak ni je. Power station. Macam powerbank, tapi jauh lebih besar. Boleh hidupkan lampu, cas telefon, siap boleh pasang kipas.”

Sound: whoosh on the cut-away; rising hum; tick per cable; fan spin-up

Camera: 37–47: into the room, 3/4 view above the box for the cut-away, then pull back to show lamp, phones and fan all powered.

07: Jiran tumpang cas
47–54.6s · still at 52.6 s

The neighbours walk up the street and gather at the front of the lit house. Their dark phones turn green one by one with small bursts.

“Jiran-jiran pun datang tumpang cas. Satu rumah, tolong satu taman.”

Sound: footsteps; a bright chime per phone

Camera: 47–54.6: pull back out to the front of the house as the neighbours gather.

08: Elektrik balik
54.6–60s · still at 57.6 s

HARI 7. Camera rises to the opening wide shot. The street lights and windows come back in a wave, the sky returns to sunset, everyone walks home. Last frame matches the first.

“Blackout tujuh hari mungkin tak jadi. Tapi blackout satu malam? Selalu. Link kat beg kuning.”

Sound: power-up swell, lamps buzzing on, final chime; ends on the evening hum from frame 0

Camera: 54.6–60: rise to the opening wide shot as the lights come back; the last frame matches the first.

Risks and unclear points

  1. The script is my draft. Approve or edit the wording before you record. Timings move to your real voice afterwards.
  2. Product claims stay modest on purpose. The film only shows a lamp, phones and a fan, and says "lampu, cas telefon, kipas". It never mentions mAh, a fridge, or powering a whole house. Line 5 ("dah tiga malam") is believable only for an LED lamp plus phones, which is why the fan starts in the explanation and not on day 1.
  3. The box is a generic model (black, handle, LCD, two sockets), not a copy of the listing photo and with no brand name. If you want it to match the 800W unit more closely, send a clear product photo. A real product photo can go on your CapCut end card.
  4. Tone. This is a what-if story in the bright explainer look, not a horror look. Nights are deep blue with warm candle and lamp light so it stays readable on a phone. Say so if you want it darker.
  5. Disclosure. Switch on TikTok's commercial-content label when you post, since the video promotes an affiliate link.
  6. Voiceover and subtitles are assumed to be added by you in CapCut, as with the lift and ship films. Say so if you want burned-in one-word captions instead.
  7. Render time is about the same as the lift film (v2 pipeline); night scenes do not add cost.

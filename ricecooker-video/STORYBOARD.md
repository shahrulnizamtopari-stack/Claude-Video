ink outlines, one blue accent). Phrase captions with a blue select-box on the spoken word, chapter title on top, live HUD pill,

## 5. Risks

1. **Script length:** 182 words in 60 s (about 3 words a second). If your voiceover runs over 60 s, I either trim a line or let the video run a little longer. I won't speed the voice up.
2. **Close-up at 16 s:** the power wire and lever cross the front of the frame. I'll move the wire behind the thermostat during the build.
3. **Split screen (22–38 s):** the two cookers are fairly small on a phone. If it looks too small in the preview, I'll crop tighter on the lower half (pot + thermostat).
4. **Lid:** the open lid behind the title reads as a white circle. It can be removed or tilted further back.
5. **Simplified mechanism:** the thermostat is drawn simplified (one ferrite, one magnet, one spring). The note on screen says so.
